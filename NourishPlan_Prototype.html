Mushroom Risotto

Instructions

A classic Italian comfort dish made with arborio rice, wild mushrooms, parmesan, and a rich vegetable broth. Creamy, earthy, and deeply satisfying — perfect for a cozy weeknight dinner.

Serves 4 people
Arborio Rice1.5 cups
Wild Mushrooms300g
Vegetable Broth4 cups
Parmesan Cheese100g
Unsalted Butter50g
White Wine½ cup
Shallots2 medium
Fresh Thyme1 bunch
1

Heat the vegetable broth in a saucepan over medium heat. Keep warm throughout cooking.

2

In a large pan, melt butter over medium heat. Sauté shallots until translucent, about 3 minutes.

3

Add mushrooms and cook until golden, about 5 minutes. Set aside half for garnish.

4

Add arborio rice and toast for 2 minutes. Pour in white wine and stir until absorbed.

5

Add warm broth one ladle at a time, stirring constantly until each addition is absorbed. Continue for 18–20 minutes until rice is al dente.
